Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Suno Companion PRO ("we," "us," or "the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data when you use our Chrome Extension.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We believe in absolute data minimization.

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).
  • No External Tracking: We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics (like Google Analytics).
  • No Account Required: You do not need to create an account with us to use the Extension.

3. Permissions and Local Data

To provide our professional engineering features, the Extension requires specific Chrome permissions:

  • storage: Used solely to save your "Prompt Vault" entries. This data stays on your local device within your Chrome Profile.
  • downloads: Used only to rename and organize your Suno tracks when you initiate a download. We do not monitor your other browser downloads.
  • tabs / Host Permissions: Used to inject the DAW-style interface into suno.com. No data from your browser sessions is sent to us or any third party.

4. Third-Party Services

The Extension interacts with Suno.com. While our Extension enhances your experience, your use of Suno.com is governed by Suno's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. We do not have access to your Suno account credentials.

5. Data Security

Because all data is stored locally via chrome.storage.local, your data is as secure as your browser/computer. We do not have "backdoor" access to your saved prompts or downloaded files.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or Chrome Web Store requirements. Updates will be posted within the Extension's store listing and on this page.

7. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the Chrome Web Store support link or visit sunocompanionpro.com.
